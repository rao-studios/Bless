Bless: a person between the model and the patient

On holding a model's reply until someone who answers for it has read it.

A patient asks a question in their clinic's app, and a language model drafts the answer. The patient's doctor is responsible for what the patient is told, so the draft cannot go out on its own. Bless holds it.

The draft becomes a petition at a gate. A reviewer reads it and blesses it, amends it or withholds it, and signs the ruling with a key of their own. Only a release crosses the gate, and every step is written to a ledger that can prove it later.

One wrapper, any model

Bless is a Swift package. Its pattern is one type, Blessed, which wraps any model that answers chat requests. A host that called the model now calls Blessed, and the call returns only once a reviewer has ruled.

The model can be Mistral, OpenAI, a model on the same Mac, or AII's Sewn. A system in another language can speak the same format: the records are plain JSON, and their signed bytes are written down in the integration guide.
