The lexicon

On the words Bless uses, each as the code spells it.

Blessed
The pattern as one type: a model wrapped so that every reply it writes is held at the Gate, and only the release comes back.
canonical bytes
The fixed layout a seal signs and a ledger hash covers, named by a domain tag such as bless/verdict/1. Not JSON.
digest
The SHA-256 of a text's UTF-8 bytes, exactly as given. The ledger names texts only by their digests. In the code, Sha256.
Draft
What a host hands the Gate: the proposed reply, the conversation it answers, and its provenance.
Gate
Where replies wait: the actor that holds petitions, applies verdicts, lapses deadlines and hands out releases.
lapse
What a policy does at a deadline: hold, withhold or releaseFallback. A lapse never sends the model's draft.
Ledger
The append-only audit log, one event per transition, each hash covering the one before it. It holds ids, digests and codes, never words.
Petition
A reply held for review, with its context, recipient, reviewers, policy and verdicts.
Policy
The rules one petition is held under: quorum, deadline, lapse, whether amendments are allowed, the context window, and whether seals are required.
InferenceProvider
Anything that answers a chat request, whole or as a stream. Bless's only demand of a model.
quorum
How many reviewers must bless the current text. An amendment starts the count again.
Release
The only thing a host may deliver: the final text, its digest, and the sealed verdicts that allowed it.
Ruling
A reviewer's decision: bless, amend or withhold.
Seal
A reviewer's Ed25519 signature over a verdict's canonical bytes.
standing
Where a petition is: pending, or one of four final standings.
Verdict
One reviewer's ruling on one petition, pinned to the digest of the words they read.
