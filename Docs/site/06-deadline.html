What happens at the deadline?

On what a policy decides when no reviewer answers in time.

A patient should not wait forever, and a clinic should not send an unread reply because no one answered. A policy's deadline settles it in advance. At the deadline, the policy decides, not a reviewer.

There are three choices: hold, withhold, releaseFallback. Hold keeps the petition waiting for a reviewer. Withhold expires it and sends nothing. A fallback expires it and sends a fixed holding message the clinic wrote in advance.

The default is to hold

A policy with no settings holds at its deadline: onLapse is hold. Nothing unreviewed is ever released by default. A clinic that wants a holding message must say so, and must write the message.

No timer inside

The Gate owns no timer. A host can run sweep on its own schedule. Every call that reads a petition lapses it first when it is due, and a caller waiting with a deadline wakes itself when the deadline passes. Correctness rests on none of the three alone.

For engineers: where each piece lives
  • The deadline-lapses trail follows a sweep, the lapse rule and the self-waking wait.
  • The clock and the sleeper are injected, so GateWaitingTests moves time by hand and never sleeps.
  • The playground ticks sweep once a second while a reply is held.
